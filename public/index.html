      Evidence receipt

      SHA-256 fingerprints of the exact policy text and this report. Keep the receipt with your compliance records: anyone holding the same text can recompute the hash and show the policy was not changed after the check.

      How it works

      Twelve requirements drawn from the COPPA Rule (16 CFR Part 312, as amended in 2025), GDPR Articles 8, 12, 13 and 17, and the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code. The list is fixed in code, so every policy gets the same test.

      Claude reads the policy once and returns a verdict for each requirement, the clause it relied on, a plain-English reason and a fix. Kidproof then searches the policy for every quote and marks any it cannot find, so an invented clause shows up.

      The receipt hashes the policy and the report in your browser. It is an automated first pass for indie developers and parents, not legal advice: have a lawyer review anything you ship to children.
